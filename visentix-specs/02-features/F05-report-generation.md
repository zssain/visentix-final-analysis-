# F05 — Report Generation (12 Sections, Snapshots, PDF)

**Status:** shipped (section gaps per MVP plan Workstream B) · **Release:** R1 · **Depends on:** F04, F06, design-system.md, business-logic.md §2/§5

## Purpose
Assemble the 12-section audit-firm-grade report from derived intelligence objects, **as a story a non-specialist can follow end to end**, freeze it as an immutable snapshot (Analyst + Advisor layers both frozen), render interactively at `/reports/:assessmentId` with PDF parity, and guarantee byte-identical re-pulls.

## Sections

**Stored blocks (the payload — unchanged).** Cover · Executive Summary · Risk Dashboard · Benchmark Intelligence · Regulator Exposure (9×8 heatmap) · Disclosure Findings · Compound Risk · Benchmark Language (side-by-side clause diff) · Recommendations · Risk Reduction · Traceability · Trend & Emerging Risk. Every snapshot — including every snapshot frozen before the regrouping below — still carries these twelve numbered blocks and still regenerates identically (Hard Rule 6). Their `section-N` anchors are preserved.

**Presented structure (owner-decided 2026-08-31) — six parts + an appendix.** The twelve blocks were organized by *formula*, not by what a reader wants to know: four separate numbered sections each existed to present one score and a visual, so "how am I doing" cost four page-turns; the two halves of our actual positioning — what comparable organizations disclose, and what we therefore recommend — sat in different sections; and Traceability, which is machinery, held a numbered slot equal in weight to the findings (DDR-011). **Grouping is a presentation concern only — never a payload change.**

| Part | Reader's question | Stored blocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Cover & Scope | What am I holding, and what was assessed? | 1 |
| 2 Executive Summary | What is the short version? | 2 |
| 3 Where You Stand | How am I doing, and compared with whom? | 3, 4, 5, 7 |
| 4 What We Found | What specifically is different about this notice? | 6 |
| 5 What Peers Do, What We Recommend | What do comparable organizations write, and what should I do? | 8, 9, 10 |
| 6 What's Changing | What moved, and what is coming? | 12 |
| Appendix · Traceability & Method | How was every figure produced? | 11 |

Rules: a part renders only if at least one of its blocks is present, so a legacy snapshot shows honest absence rather than an empty heading; a part opens a page, and blocks inside it flow on with sub-headings rather than each claiming a page; the part list is duplicated in exactly two places — `web/src/report/sectionGroups.ts` and `renderer.py::_REPORT_PARTS` — which **must** be kept in step or PDF parity breaks.

**Part 5 is the positioning.** "Here is how comparable organizations word this; here is what we suggest; here is what it would move" is the report's argument, and it reads as one thought only when the peer language, the recommendation, and the priority sit together. Note that Risk Reduction's own `risk_reduction_delta` is **null forever** by the addendum below (no formula exists), so as a standalone section it was framed around a value the specs forbid computing.

### The narrative arc (owner-decided 2026-08-31)
The 12 sections are the evidence; they are not, by themselves, a story. A reader who opens the PDF cold must be told — in common language, before any number — what they are holding, and must be left at the end knowing what it meant and where to go next. The governed 12-section sequence is **unchanged**; the arc is carried by the existing non-numbered front and closing matter:

1. **Opening — "what this is", in common language.** The Cover carries a short plain-English statement of what the report is and what the intelligence engine does: that Visentix compares this organization's public privacy notice against the notices of similar organizations and against regulatory and enforcement signals, and reports where it stands and how confident that comparison is. Register: no house acronyms (design-system §4), no formula names, no jargon — a privacy-adjacent reader with no Visentix context is the target. This is **authored static copy**, not generated prose, and still passes the banned-term filter.
2. **Scope — what was actually assessed.** The Assessment Scope front matter (below) immediately follows, in the same plain register: the source and version assessed, the dates, the peer group it was compared against, and what was *not* in scope. This is the front half of the revised DDR-007 bookend pair.
3. **Body — Sections 1–12,** unchanged.
4. **Wrap-up — what it meant.** The Next Steps page opens with a short **closing summary** that restates, in the same plain language as the Cover, the two or three things this assessment actually found and what changes them — drawn **only** from the frozen snapshot's own findings and scores (never re-derived, never a new claim, never a number that does not already appear in a section). Then the existing operationalization guidance.
5. **Deeper context — the link out.** The closing matter carries a single explicit **"Read more about how this works"** link to the public methodology/site, in both the interactive report and the PDF (printed as a resolvable URL, never a bare "click here"). One link, one destination — not a marketing block.
6. **Disclosure — the closing bookend.** A single Disclosure block closes the deliverable, replacing the per-surface "Intelligence, not legal advice" mark (revised DDR-007). See Guardrails below.

**Source summary (RPT-007) — what the judgments rest on.** Per-figure lineage answers *where did this number come from*. It does not answer the question a third-party reader — a board, an auditor, a prospect — actually opens with: **which evidence is carrying the conclusions, and where is it thin?** The Traceability & Method part therefore opens with a source summary: one row per headline judgment (what it rests on · how strong that base is · why), followed by named strengths and named limitations of the evidence base.

Rules: it is **assembled and frozen into the snapshot** (`assembly.py::_build_source_summary`), never computed at render (DIR-008). Every value is counted or copied from data already present — nothing is estimated, and a missing input yields an explicit absence rather than a default that reads like a measurement (Hard Rule 7). **Evidence-base strength is not the score**: a good score resting on a small cohort still reports a limited base, and collapsing the two would hide exactly what the block exists to show. One limitation is always stated because it is always true — this assessment reads the published notice, not internal practice, contracts, or systems.

*Analogical basis, stated as such:* the shape follows ICD 203's source-summary requirement (identify the sources and methodologies judgments rest on; give a holistic assessment of the strengths and weaknesses of the source base and which sources drive the key judgments) and the IIA's placement of scope limitations inside the scope section. **No source in the language research concerns B2B SaaS privacy reports** — the transfer is by analogy, and the placement question (appendix versus front matter) is not settled by evidence. It currently sits at the head of the appendix; surfacing a condensed form in the Cover/Scope front matter is an open follow-up, not a decided design.

**Assessment Scope front matter (RPT-006 / INT-10):** a non-numbered block within the Cover keeps the governed 12-section sequence unchanged. It freezes the assessed source/version/dates, organization profile, distinct footprint and selected-law scope, declared data categories/practices, per-field provenance, and benchmark definition. Legacy snapshots render honest absence and are never back-filled.

**Closing matter (added from Appendix H prototype):** after Section 12, the report carries a **Next Steps page** (how to operationalize the findings: share internally, build an action plan, validate & re-assess, upgrade to Continuous Monitoring) and a **branded back cover** ("Thank you for your trust" + contact + confidentiality line). Guardrail notes: closing matter is marketing-adjacent copy and still passes the banned-term filter; the monitoring upsell is phrased as capability ("ongoing visibility"), never as risk pressure; the confidentiality line matches the cover's. Closing matter is frozen into the snapshot like every other page.

## Behavior
1. **Narrative engine:** LLM rephrases finding narratives → verification pass → banned-term filter (with source-excerpt handling) → deterministic fallback on failure. Advisor Note prose frozen into snapshot; **never regenerated at render** (M-05).
2. **Snapshot lifecycle:** draft (gold ribbon + diagonal DRAFT watermark, `instant_draft` mode) → SME approval → approved (teal Reproducible ribbon). Provenance ribbon on every report page from real snapshot fields (M-09).
3. **Furniture:** lineage drawer on every number, Codex tooltip on every code (PDF appends Codex appendix), **scope statement at the front + one Disclosure block at the end** in place of the per-surface "Intelligence, not legal advice" mark (revised DDR-007 — *the mark is removed only in the same change that ships both bookends*; needs expert + engineer joint approval), reader-register toggle — **Executive / Practitioner / Plain-language**, flag-gated (OD-02 Decided 2026-07-27, ai_reviewed). The framework-crosswalk copy in the report is **descriptive-only** (OD-01 Decided 2026-07-27, ai_reviewed). On mobile the Advisor layer may lead with the two OD-03 mitigations (thumb-reachable View Switch, full-screen lineage sheet) — OD-03 Decided 2026-07-27 (ai_reviewed). All three pending human owner confirmation.
4. **Score presentation (owner-decided 2026-08-31).** Every score in the report leads with its **band label** and its **plain-language peer position**; the figure is secondary, reachable in the lineage drawer, on hover, and in the Traceability section — stored, frozen, and never removed (design-system §2; intelligence-logic §3). A named measure ("Overall") shows the **same value, band, label, and color** on the Cover dial, the Risk Dashboard, every section body, the PDF, and the monitoring hero for a given snapshot; precision and rounding for a named measure are decided once in `scoreBands.ts` and reused, never re-derived per section. Peer-position words come from the governed vocabulary (intelligence-logic §3) and **do not render until OD-14 closes** — until then the band label and cohort context carry the comparison alone.
5. **Gap statements are general and paraphrased (owner-decided 2026-08-31).** Where the report says what is missing relative to the cohort, it says so directionally and in common language — the shape of the gap ("most organizations like yours state how long they keep data; yours does not yet"), and, where the cohort supports it, the *kind of language* stronger peers use for it. Constraints, all binding: the paraphrase governs **wording only**, and every figure behind it is the real live-queried cohort value with its n and as-of date (Hard Rule 7 unchanged — no illustrative shares, ever); bucketed phrasing comes only from an expert-governed bucket map applied to a real figure; the statement is suppressed below `LOW_CONFIDENCE_COHORT_N` rather than softened; and it stays descriptive of the cohort, never a requirement asserted against the reader. Modality follows business-logic §2 — **must** only from a citable source carrying that modality (blocked on OD-16), **should** for peer norm and authored recommendations.
6. **Regulator Exposure heatmap is explorable (owner-decided 2026-08-31).** A heatmap cell is a control, not a swatch: activating one (click, and keyboard-accessible) opens a panel giving that cell's plain-language read — what the row and column mean without acronyms, this organization's standing in that cell as a band, how that compares with the peer cohort (cohort n + as-of date), the findings evidencing it, and the lineage affordance. The panel is **served from the frozen snapshot** and never re-derived at render (DIR-008 / Hard Rule 6). **Unevidenced cells stay neutral and say so plainly** — an empty cell opens to "no disclosure language found for this area", never to a fabricated comparison; cells whose cohort falls below `LOW_CONFIDENCE_COHORT_N` open without the peer comparison.
7. **Risk horizons (PROPOSED — OD-15).** Section 12 (Trend & Emerging Risk) is where the horizon distinction lands: bills under consideration are **emerging**, enacted-but-not-yet-effective is **scheduled**, law in force is **current**, regulator action is **demonstrated**, private litigation is **contested** (intelligence-logic §13). Horizons are narrative context and sequencing only — **no score, weight, or ranking may depend on them** until an expert-approved formula version says otherwise. Nothing ships here until OD-15 closes.
8. **Benchmark Language:** side-by-side diff, gold=exemplar-adds / gray-strikethrough=weaker phrasing, show-differences-only toggle, honest cohort footer (live n, date, low-confidence rule).
9. **PDF renderer:** parity with interactive view; double-pull of same snapshot is byte-identical.
10. **Evidence integrity:** live assembly uses one substantive-clause read for heatmap density, representative language, findings, and traceability. A stored scored-clause-count mismatch is visible and suppresses maturity/benchmark presentation. Unevidenced heatmap cells remain neutral. Comparator language requires approved status, same-domain taxonomy, governed semantic-similarity floor, and a stored maturity signal at least as strong as the assessed clause.

## Guardrails & confidence
Banned-term filter is a hard gate on all narrative content including Recommendations/Risk Reduction static copy — and equally on the Cover's "what this is" statement, the closing summary, and the Disclosure block. Low-VCI items suppressed before assembly. Every displayed figure traces to a derived_data_item.

**Bookended disclosure (revised DDR-007, owner-decided 2026-08-31 — needs expert + engineer joint approval).** The per-surface "Intelligence, not legal advice" mark is replaced by two authored blocks frozen into the snapshot: the **scope statement** at the front (what was assessed, against what, as of when, and what was out of scope) and one **Disclosure** at the end (what this intelligence is and is not, how it should and should not be used, and the confidence and cohort caveats that apply). Rationale: repeated on every card the mark was skimmed past; stated once where the reader starts and once where they finish, it is read. **The mark is removed only in the change that ships both blocks** — a build that drops it without them fails review. Nothing else in the guardrail stack moves: banned-term filtering, VCI suppression, exposure-only vocabulary, and the source-excerpt exception are unchanged (business-logic §2).

**Register.** All customer-facing report copy minimizes acronyms per design-system §4 — house acronyms (RSS, PGMS, DSI, AIGMS, VCI…) are never the label a reader sees, only a secondary reference in the lineage drawer, Traceability, or Methodology. Finding codes remain chips with their Codex tooltip. Prose describes position and opportunity rather than deficiency in the reader (no negative framing, business-logic §2) — without hiding a real gap or dressing up an honest absence.

### Data (new — amends schema.md)
`recommendation_evidence(id uuid pk, finding_id fk→risk_finding, assessment_id fk→privacy_notice(notice_id), obligation_refs jsonb [{obligation_id, similarity, matched_terms}], exemplar_clause_id fk→disclosure_clause null (SME-**approved** only), enforcement_refs jsonb [{enforcement_id, similarity}] — **resolved enforcement only, max 2**, risk_reduction_delta numeric null, formula_version_id text, confidence numeric, generated_at)`.
Reads: `clause_obligation` (obligation context — the F05 addendum's obligation source; scope recorded in `logs/eval/obligation_match_scope.json`), `disclosure_clause` (`is_exemplar=true, exemplar_status='approved'`), `enforcement_record` (`resolution_status='resolved'`).
Also documents the **live-but-undocumented `recommendation_library`** table (`finding_type_code, title, body_template, severity_bucket`) — the origin of Recommendations content.

### Assembly (hook: `report/assembly.py`)
For each **confirmed** finding in the report, `assemble_report` builds one `recommendation_evidence` row and embeds it in the snapshot payload:
- **Obligation context** — from `clause_obligation` for the finding's cited clauses (obligation + similarity + matched_terms). Copy register is **verbatim from `obligation_match.py`**: *"Matches are EXPOSURE CONTEXT only — never a legal conclusion. Unverified obligations (effective_date=NULL) carry reduced confidence."*
- **Exemplar** — a single **SME-approved** clause (`exemplar_status='approved'`) for the finding's domain, or an **honest absence line** (no approved exemplar for this domain yet). Never a non-approved exemplar.
- **Enforcement precedent** — up to **2** rows from **resolved** enforcement only (`resolution_status='resolved'`), each with regulator + year + similarity.
- **`risk_reduction_delta` — NULL forever.** intelligence-logic.md defines **no** risk-reduction formula; the column exists only for a future expert-defined formula. It is never computed or invented here (MUST NOT).
- **Honest absence, three distinct claims:** an org whose cohort was **not** matched (out of `obligation_match_scope`) shows *"obligation context not yet available"*; a clause with matches below the 0.35 floor shows *"no related obligations above the similarity threshold"*; no approved exemplar shows *"no approved exemplar for this domain yet."* These are never conflated.

### API
`GET /assessments/{id}/findings/{fid}/evidence` → the frozen stack **from the snapshot** (org-scoped; 403 cross-org). Never assembled at render. Every element echoes its refs for the lineage drawer.
