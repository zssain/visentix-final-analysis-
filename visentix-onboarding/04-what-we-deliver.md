# What We Deliver — The Deliverables, in Plain English

This is what a customer (or partner) actually receives for their money, product by product. Written so it can be shared with a prospect as-is.

---

## Deliverable 1 — The Privacy Notice Intelligence Report

*The flagship. One company, one notice, one deep look.*

A 12-part report, delivered as an interactive online report and a matching PDF, designed to feel like a top consulting firm's deliverable — because a board member might read it.

What's inside, section by section:

1. **Cover** — your company, the overall score, the confidence dial, and the report's snapshot ID (its permanent fingerprint).
2. **Executive Summary** — the whole story in one page, written for someone with three minutes.
3. **Risk Dashboard** — your scores across all eight disclosure areas at a glance.
4. **Benchmark Intelligence** — where you rank against your true peers, with the peer-group size shown honestly.
5. **Regulator Exposure** — a heatmap of which regulators care about which of your weak spots.
6. **Disclosure Findings** — each specific issue, shown two ways: the precise machine analysis and a plain-English advisor's note.
7. **Compound Risk** — where several weaknesses combine into something bigger.
8. **Benchmark Language** — your actual clause side-by-side with an anonymized example of what stronger peers say. The differences are highlighted. This is usually the most persuasive page in the report.
9. **Recommendations** — what to improve first.
10. **Risk Reduction** — the expected effect of acting on the recommendations.
11. **Traceability** — the receipts: how every number was produced.
12. **Trend & Emerging Risk** — how your position is moving over time (once you have history).

Three promises attach to every report:

- **Reviewed by a human expert** before you see it (unless you asked for an instant draft, which is clearly watermarked as such).
- **Every number is clickable** — the evidence behind it is one tap away.
- **Frozen forever** — pull the same report in five years, it's identical to the byte.

And one honest limit, printed right on it: *this is intelligence, not legal advice.* We tell you how you compare and what regulators focus on; your lawyers tell you what's legal.

## Deliverable 2 — The Continuous Monitoring Platform

*The subscription. The system that keeps watching after the report.*

- **A live dashboard**: your overall score with its trend line, all eight domain scores with their movement since last time. Colors follow one rule: improving = teal, worsening = red — regardless of which direction the number moved.
- **A change feed**: a timestamped diary of everything that mattered — your notice changed, a score moved (shown as the numbers, e.g. 41 → 38), a regulator made a relevant announcement, your peer group was refreshed.
- **Alerts**: when something serious happens, you're told, with the full finding one click away.
- **Quiet honesty**: if nothing changed, it says "no changes since [date]" — a calm statement, not an error. On your very first assessment it says "baseline established" instead of faking a trend.

## Deliverable 3 — White-Label Intelligence (for partners)

*For consulting, law, audit, and advisory firms who want to offer this to their clients under their own name.*

- A **partner portal** with a separate workspace per client.
- **Your branding** on the report templates.
- **Data feeds and APIs**: benchmark data, regulator trend summaries, and risk-signal data — always aggregated and anonymized, with our confidence ratings and methodology notes attached, and clear permitted-use terms.
- Every number in a partner-branded report is the same number our own product would show — one engine, no side calculations.

## Deliverable 4 — The Quarterly Global Privacy Intelligence Report

*The published, public-facing report. Our megaphone and the industry's briefing.*

Each quarter: industry benchmark rankings, the most common disclosure gaps across the market, AI disclosure trends, where regulators focused their energy, and how it all moved since last quarter. Beautiful, editorial, shareable — with a methodology page that states exactly how big the data set was and how every statistic was produced. Everything in it is anonymized and drawn only from groups large enough that no company can be identified.
