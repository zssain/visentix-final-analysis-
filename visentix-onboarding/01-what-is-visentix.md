# What Is Visentix? (The App Brief, in Plain English)

## The problem

Every company with a website has a privacy notice — that long page explaining what data they collect and what they do with it. Almost nobody reads them, but regulators do, lawyers do, and increasingly, plaintiffs' law firms do. A vague or outdated privacy notice can lead to fines, lawsuits, and embarrassing headlines.

Here's the catch: a company has no good way to know **how its notice stacks up**. Is it better or worse than competitors? Does it say the things regulators have been punishing other companies for *not* saying? Has the market moved while their notice stood still? Today, answering those questions means paying a law firm a lot of money for a one-off opinion — and the answer is stale the moment it's delivered.

## The idea

Visentix reads privacy notices the way an experienced privacy analyst would — but at scale, continuously, and with receipts.

We collect thousands of public privacy notices, plus regulator announcements, enforcement actions, and lawsuits. We break every notice down into its individual statements, sort those statements into categories (data sharing, retention, consumer rights, AI use, and so on), and then compare any one company against a carefully chosen group of genuinely similar companies — its "peers."

Out of that comparison come scores, findings, and a beautiful executive report that says, in effect: *"Here's where you stand, here's what companies like you are doing that you aren't, here's what regulators have been focusing on lately, and here's exactly the sentence in your notice that made us say so."*

Think of it as **"Bloomberg for privacy risk"** — an intelligence service, not a compliance tool.

## The one sentence that defines us

> **We never say whether something is legal. We say how it compares, and how confident we are.**

This is the most important idea in the whole company. We are not lawyers, and our product never issues legal verdicts like "compliant" or "violation." Instead we talk about *exposure*, *maturity*, *benchmark position*, and *confidence*. That's not a limitation — it's the product. Lawyers and regulators trust us precisely because we stay in our lane and show our work.

## What makes us different

**1. We compare fairly.** Most tools compare a company against a crude industry average. We build a custom peer group considering industry, size, how much regulatory attention the company should expect, how sensitive its data is, and how sophisticated its privacy program appears. A ten-person shop is never graded against a Fortune 100.

**2. Every number has a receipt.** Click any score in our product and a panel slides out showing exactly where it came from: which sentence in the notice, which regulator concern, which peer group, which formula, and how confident we are. We call this *lineage*. No black boxes, ever.

**3. A human expert stands behind every report.** Before a report goes to a client, our expert reviews every finding and can confirm, edit, or dismiss it. The report you get isn't raw machine output — it's machine analysis with expert judgment on top.

**4. Reports are frozen in time.** When we deliver a report, we take a "snapshot." Pull that report again next year and it will be *identical, to the byte*. That's how professional firms work, and it's how we work.

**5. It keeps watching.** Beyond one-off reports, the platform monitors notices, regulators, and peer behavior continuously, and tells customers when something changed that matters to them.

## Who buys it

- **Companies** (privacy officers, legal, compliance) who want to know where they stand and get early warning of trouble.
- **Consulting, law, and audit firms** who want to deliver our intelligence under their own brand to their clients.
- **Executives, analysts, and regulators** who want the big-picture quarterly view of how privacy disclosure is trending across whole industries.

## The four products (one engine underneath)

1. **One-time Assessment** — a deep, benchmarked report on one company's notice.
2. **Continuous Monitoring platform** — dashboards, alerts, and trends, ongoing.
3. **White-label service** — partners deliver our intelligence under their brand.
4. **Quarterly Intelligence Report** — a published market-wide report; our megaphone.

All four are the same engine wearing different clothes. We never build a separate brain for each product — that's a founding rule.
