# Welcome to Visentix — Start Here

This folder is the plain-language guide to Visentix. It exists to do three things:

1. **Onboard new people** — engineers, experts, business folks — without them needing to decode technical specs on day one.
2. **Close the gap between developers and our expert** — so the person who understands privacy and the people who write code are always talking about the same thing.
3. **Explain to customers what they're actually buying** — in words a busy executive understands.

Everything here is written for a smart person who knows *nothing* about our codebase, and possibly nothing about privacy law either. If a document in this folder confuses you, that's a bug in the document — tell us and we'll fix the document, not you.

There is a separate, more technical folder called `visentix-specs` for people building the product. This folder never contradicts it — it just says the same things in plainer words. When in doubt, the technical specs win on details; this folder wins on understanding.

---

## What to read, in what order

**If you're a new engineer (first week):**
1. `01-what-is-visentix.md` — what we're building and why
2. `02-how-it-works.md` — the journey of a privacy notice through our system
3. `03-words-we-use.md` — the vocabulary (keep this open, always)
4. `06-the-rules-we-never-break.md` — the non-negotiables
5. `09-how-we-build.md` — our spec-driven way of working with AI
6. `08-onboarding-checklists.md` — your week-one checklist
Then move to the technical `visentix-specs` folder.

**If you're our expert, or a new expert/reviewer:**
1. `01-what-is-visentix.md`
2. `05-who-does-what.md` — especially your role and what only you can do
3. `04-what-we-deliver.md` — what your name and judgment stand behind
4. `06-the-rules-we-never-break.md` — you are the guardian of these
5. `07-customer-journeys.md` — what customers experience
6. `03-words-we-use.md` — as a reference

**If you're in sales, partnerships, or talking to customers:**
1. `01-what-is-visentix.md`
2. `04-what-we-deliver.md`
3. `07-customer-journeys.md`
4. `03-words-we-use.md`

**If you're a customer or design partner reading this:**
`01-what-is-visentix.md` and `04-what-we-deliver.md` were written with you in mind.
